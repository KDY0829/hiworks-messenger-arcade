The bundled quiz bank contains adapted and filtered Common Knowledge (Korean)
items from KorNAT. KorNAT is distributed under Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 2.0 (CC BY-NC 2.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/2.0/

The material was transformed into a compact chat multiple-choice format,
filtered for quality and prohibited question types, categorized, and assigned
local difficulty buckets. This notice does not change the license of the rest
of this repository. Commercial users must obtain appropriate permission or
replace the bundled question bank.
